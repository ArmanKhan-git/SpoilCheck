Camera (sRGB)

Always on: the main signal

Required

    Add 1–6 photos of the same fruit from different sides. On a computer you can also drop them here.

    If you know the fruit, choose it: the model then only decides fresh or spoiled, so it can't mistake one fruit for another. All photos are treated as the same fruit.

    Helps with photos taken on a table, in a kitchen or at a market. Not needed for dataset samples, which already have a white background.
